EHLNOFEY — The Ten Bands of Power

I. Weak (1–6)
Skeevers, mudcrabs, beggars, slaughterfish

II. Common (7–13)
Townsfolk, most wildlife, low-level bandits

III. Trained (14–20)
Most bandits, low-level soldiers and guards, novice mages

IV. Blooded (21–27)
Low-level Forsworn, fort bandits, low-level draugr, apprentice mages, most soldiers and guards, low-level Falmer, ghosts

V. Experienced (28–34)
Forsworn, guard and soldier captains, adept mages, Falmer, draugr, bandit chiefs, witches and hags, low-level vampires, regular Thalmor soldiers, Dwarven spiders, Vigilants, the Silver Hand

VI. Elite (35–41)
Thalmor Justiciars, Penitus Oculatus, Dark Brotherhood and Morag Tong assassins, Forsworn Briarhearts, draugr Deathlords, Falmer Shadowmasters and Warmongers, Dwarven spheres and ballistas, expert mages, the Dawnguard

VII. Powerful (42–50)
Dwarven centurions, werewolves, vampires, lesser Daedra (Seekers, Dremora, Saints and Seducers), most lesser named bosses

VIII. Fabled (51–65)
Named Dragon Priests, vampire lords, named Daedra, low-level dragons

IX. Mythic (66–81)
Dragons, greater Daedra, Harkon, the Ebony Warrior

X. Legendary (82+)
Miraak (100), Alduin (150)
